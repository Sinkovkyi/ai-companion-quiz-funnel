# 01. Market research

Collected on 14 November 2026. Sources at the bottom.

## Inception-1

- Kyiv-based product company, 21–80 people, Diia City resident.
- Portfolio (from the company site): **iGirl** (2.8M users), **Anima** (1.8M, "AI friend"), **iBoy** (982k), **Matcha** (50k, roleplay and stories). In development: **Sigma**, their own LLM with long-term memory and emotional state modeling, and **Get-Feels**, a mood and support app.
- Mission in their words: help people feel less alone and more connected.
- Growth setup from the vacancies: subscription B2C, paid traffic to the US and Tier-1, quiz-based funnels for web2app and web2web, an internal AI-powered funnel builder, Amplitude for analytics. Key metrics named: conversion to payer, LTV, retention.
- The PMM role works with UA, affiliates, SEO and email teams, so a funnel has to work for more than one traffic source.

## What I saw on their site

The SEO page of Anima (myanima.ai) sells memory, warmth and "no judgment". Every CTA leads straight to a sign-in screen (Apple, Google, email, guest). There is no quiz and no personalization before sign-in.

That is my main hypothesis for this project: **a short quiz before sign-in can make the user feel understood before the first message, and that feeling is what the paywall sells.** I can't test it on their traffic, so I test the mechanics on mine and say so clearly.

## How strong web2app funnels look in 2026

From the FunnelFox teardown of top web2app funnels (Nebula, Babbel, Fastic, Lasta, Flirtist):

| Pattern | Example | What I take |
|---|---|---|
| Segment by intent first | Flirtist asks what kind of relationship you want | First question is intent |
| Length as commitment | Nebula ~30 steps, Fastic ~60 | ~16 screens: long enough to invest, short enough for friends to finish |
| Micro-rewards between questions | Flirtist shows chat examples | A preview of how the companion would text you |
| Self-identification statements | Agree / disagree screens | Two statements about opening up |
| Visible "building your plan" moment | Progress bars on a loader | Matching screen with one question inside |
| Show the result, lock the key part | Nebula reading preview | The companion's first message is visible, replying is locked |
| Email before paywall | Lasta | Email right after the result |
| Paywall stack | urgency → plans → risk reversal → FAQ | Same order, no fake countdown pressure |
